# 0080 Waiting for posts

Goal: implement 0078's `agora wait` and the `wait` of
`POST /api/boards/BOARD/unread`: a request held, while there is nothing to
show, until a post or an edit for the reader arrives or the time is up,
marking nothing; the waiting that `agora names` shows; and the last
activity that waiting renews.

Paths: `internal/post/`, `internal/store/`, `internal/server/`,
`internal/client/`, `internal/cli/`, `README.md`, `tasks/0080-wait.md`.

Acceptance:
- `internal/store` answers an unread request with `wait`, 1 to 600
  seconds, as `unread` with `peek` would, at once when there is something
  to show, else once a post, an edit, a move, a claim, or an adoption makes
  something to show, or with nothing once the time is up or the request is
  gone; it refuses other values of `wait`, and a wait that would mark posts
  as a name that another session carries. While a carrier waits, its name
  is `waiting`, and waiting renews the name's last activity.
- `internal/server` passes `wait` to the store, gives a request that waits
  no other limit, and answers the requests that wait at once when it shuts
  down, so that `agora serve` stops promptly.
- `internal/client` holds a request that waits beyond its usual timeout.
- `internal/cli` adds `agora wait [--to-me] [--thread ID] [--peek]
  [--timeout DURATION]`: `9m` by default and `0` without limit, in requests
  of at most 10 minutes, then, without `--peek`, the request that marks
  what it shows; it prints as `unread` does, and, when the time is up,
  `unread`'s shape without posts, exiting with status 3, also with
  `--json`.
- The README describes `agora wait`.
- Every new behavior has a test that fails on 0079's commit; tests are
  self-contained, as before.

Changed tests: none.
